2. Manage Granular Consent Purposes

Modify individual data processing streams. Toggling off stipend processing will place your active fellowship on hold.

Stipend Processing & Data Sharing (Mandatory for Active Fellowship) Authorize sharing identity details with banking partners to process monthly stipends.
Media Highlights & Photo Use (Optional) Consent to featuring your project highlights or photographs in the Foundation's public reports.
SMS Notifications & Alerts (Optional) Opt-in to automated SMS notifications regarding class timetables and stipend dispatch updates.
External Reporting & Donor Audits (Optional) Consent to sharing aggregated or coded metrics with Inuka Foundation donor boards.

3. Compliance & Revocation Center

Under Section 40 of the Kenya Data Protection Act, you can withdraw your consent at any time or request profile erasure.

⚠️ Warning: Toggling off data sharing or choosing to withdraw consent will place your stipend enrollment status "On Hold" immediately.
